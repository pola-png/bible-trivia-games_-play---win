1. Introduction

Welcome to Bible Trivia Quiz: Play & Win ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy discloses our practices concerning the collection, use, processing, and disclosure of your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide authentication, deliver educational Bible trivia gameplay, track non-monetary rewards, and fulfill physical merchandise redemptions:

  • Account Identification: When you sign in (via Google Sign-In or email), we receive your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and unique Firebase User ID (UID).
  • Gameplay & Session Data: We record your quiz statistics, passed stages, correct/timed-out answer counts, and question history to prevent question repetition for at least 10 days across our 100,000+ question bank.
  • Points Ledger & Rewards: We maintain a ledger of your promotional trivia points, daily attendance streaks, and referral bonuses.
  • Delivery Address (Optional & On-Demand): If you choose to redeem physical products from our in-app Rewards Shop, we collect recipient name, contact phone number, state, city, and street address solely to dispatch physical goods via domestic courier services.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Anonymous device model, operating system version, and crash logs collected automatically via Google Firebase and Google Mobile Ads SDK for stability and app performance.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your identity and safeguard your account against unauthorized access.
  • To calculate trivia quiz scores and ensure anti-cheat fair play.
  • To process physical merchandise delivery orders placed in the in-app shop.
  • To display contextual, native, banner, and rewarded video ads via Google AdMob.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.
No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or commercial advertisers.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & SDKs

Our application integrates the following third-party software development kits (SDKs), each governed by their respective privacy standards:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore): Used for secure cloud database storage and authentication. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to deliver banner ads, native cards, and opt-in rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID) and interaction data to serve ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies. Google AdMob Policies.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All network communications between our app and Google Firebase / Google AdMob servers are protected with Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption in transit. User profile records are stored in secure Google Cloud Firestore data centers with granular access control rules.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users have the right to permanently erase their account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings & Rules → tap Delete Account & Data → confirm to immediately and permanently delete your user profile, points, and delivery addresses.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access, you can submit an instant deletion request via our public web portal: https://woven-art-402909.web.app/delete-account.html.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our application is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data without verified parental consent, we promptly delete such data from our servers.
